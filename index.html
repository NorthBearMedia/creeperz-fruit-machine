Season 2 rules

How to win, how you start, what your spin means, and the Season 1 loopholes that are now shut.

Draft · agree before launch

How to win

Score = cash + land, kit and buildings at the price you paid + animals and stock at this month's selling price − loans − your starting money

  • The highest score at the final save wins.
  • The finish is a real date and time, agreed before launch night, so nobody can fast-forward to the end.
  • Map buildings that come with land count as £0. So do solar panels, wind turbines and generators, same as Season 1.
  • Robin Hood tax: after each weekly table, the team in front pays 10% of the gap to the team behind.
  • League table and report cards every week, all checked from the save.

Starting out

  • £5m per team, the same for both. The tight-budget twist starts on £4m.
  • No land, kit or buildings. You buy everything after the spin.
  • The zones get marked on the map once it's been spun. Land in your zone only.
  • Wildcard cash, fields and buildings are added by the admin.
  • Loans are allowed, but they come straight off your score.
  • Settings as Season 1: Easy economy, seasonal growth, 1 day a month, time locked at 3x, normal fuel, weeds on, ploughing, lime and stones off, crop destruction off.

What your spin means

  • Tractor brand: every tractor must be your brand. Combines, loaders, telehandlers, lorries and tools can be any brand.
  • Kit colour: paint everything you can in your colour.
  • Zone: all your land, and your yard, sit in your zone.
  • Crop: the only crop you grow (No animals gives you a second). Grass is fine for feeding your own animals, but not for selling.
  • Animal: the only animal you keep. No dairy, so no dairy cows, goats or buffalo.
  • Production: the only production you run, up to 2 of it. The Free production wildcard adds one more that uses your crop or animal.
  • Twist: lasts all season. Twist break pauses it for one game month.
  • Contracts and forestry are open to both teams.

Building

  • Everything goes within 130 m of your yard's centre point (about 13 acres). Pick the spot in your zone on launch night. The small-yard twist makes it 90 m (about 6 acres).
  • No more than 2 of any one building (the same shop item).
  • No more than 2 animal buildings. Beehives are the exception: as many as fit in your yard.
  • Lights and deco: up to 60 per team. The Xbox has an object limit for the whole server.
  • AI workers are allowed, unless your twist caps them.
  • Only the admin removes or moves map objects.

Mods

  • Season 1's kit and deco mods carry over, minus the ones below.
  • The list is fixed on launch night. Changes need all four to agree.
  • Out: Larger Animal Barns, Farm Fill Stations, and anything else that buys, sells or pays out at its own prices, like pallet shops or subsidies.
  • Modded tractors count for your brand, same as base-game ones.
  • Deco mods are fine, inside the deco limit.

The admin

  • Claude checks the save, posts the tables and puts every ruling in the chat.
  • The save only gets edited for game bugs, the Robin Hood tax and wildcards. Every edit gets posted.
  • Game bugs get refunded. Your own mistakes don't: sell back through the game as normal.
  • No save restores unless all four agree.

Season 1 loopholes, now shut

The loophole

Season 2 rule

Selling the free buildings that come with land

No profit from map buildings. Any sale money comes off your score.

Buying land, stripping it and selling it back

No selling land.

Buying goods and selling them straight on

Only sell what your own farm produced (crops, animals, products, timber), plus contract pay.

Mods that buy or sell at their own prices

Not installed. Base-game shops and sell points only.

Bigger-barn mods packing in thousands of animals

Base-game barns only, and no more than 2 animal buildings.

Stacking the same building over and over

No more than 2 of any one building.

Moving money between farms

No transfers. Only the admin moves money, for the tax and wildcards.

Sitting idle or sleeping to push the clock on

Log off when you're not playing. No sleeping.

Refunds for your own mistakes

Only game bugs get refunded.

Crops and animals not counting until sold

They count at this month's price, so there's no last-day sell-off.

Players landing on the wrong farm

Stay on your own team's farm. The admin fixes permissions.

The server hitting the Xbox object limit

Yard limit, 2 of any building, a deco limit, and only the admin removes map objects.
